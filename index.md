# DOAS Drawing Matcher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026  
Publisher: Jun Hong  
Contact: jhongng.1212.dveloper@gmail.com

## Information processed

DOAS Drawing Matcher processes drawing filenames and relative paths from files or folders you choose, screenshots you choose or paste, recognized text, and BOM identifiers, descriptions and selection information from the DOAS tab you explicitly connect.

This information is used to compare drawings with BOM rows, display validation results, select matching checkboxes when permitted, and export an audit when you request it. PDF matching uses filenames; it does not read the contents of PDF pages.

## Local processing and sharing

Matching and screenshot recognition run on your device. OCR code, WebAssembly and English language data are bundled with the extension. The extension does not upload your drawings, screenshots or BOM data to the publisher. It implements no analytics, advertising, sale of data or third-party tracking.

Checkbox changes occur within DOAS. The DOAS website may process those interactions according to its own policies. Browser installation and update services, and GitHub's hosting of this policy page, are governed by their providers' policies.

## Retention and deletion

Working inputs and reports remain in the open extension workspace. Closing or refreshing it clears that working state. The OCR library may cache engine or language resources in browser-managed storage. Browser data controls can clear those resources.

Exported audits remain in your download location until you delete them. They may contain identifiers, filenames, relative paths, descriptions and mapping decisions. Original drawing files are not modified or deleted by the extension.

## Permissions

The activeTab permission grants temporary access to the tab where you click the extension. The scripting permission allows bundled scripts to inspect the connected DOAS BOM and operate validated row checkboxes. The extension does not request general browsing-history access.
